# SCOPE foundation

This page is adapted directly from [neuqrui/SCOPE-page](https://github.com/neuqrui/SCOPE-page), as requested by the repository owner.

- `base.css`: original `styles.css`.
- `experience.css`, `refinements.css`, `release.css`: original styles under `experience/`.
- `motion.js`: adapted `experience/experience.js`; retains ambient particles, cursor trails, wordmark bursts, section reveals, and motion preferences.
- `navigation.js`: original `experience/navigation.js`; retains the responsive contents panel and scroll tracking.
- `problem.js`: adapted `experience/results.js`; keeps the chart DOM structure, animated bars, interactive legends, keyboard controls, and viewport-triggered animation, with DeShortcut-Align Figure 1 data.

All scientific content is from DeShortcut-Align. The problem chart transcribes the labels printed in Figure 1 to one decimal, comparing the full post-training template and no-template conditions. Pre-training-template bars remain visible in the expandable original figure.
