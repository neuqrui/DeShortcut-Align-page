03TRAINING DYNAMICS

Keep the learning signal alive.

Standard GRPO progresses from formatting shortcuts to lexical shortcuts, then loses its optimization signal. DeShortcut-Align changes that trajectory.

01 / 0–40

Formatting shortcut

Reward improves quickly as the model learns a template-to-refusal association.

02 / 40–100

Lexical shortcut

The template gap narrows, but sensitive words increasingly trigger false refusals.

03 / 100+

Gradient starvation

Saturated rewards leave little within-group variation for further refinement.

EXTENDED EXPERIMENTS

More backbones. More checks.

Open an experiment to see its full results.

Three training seeds · Qwen3-1.7B

Across seeds 0, 1, and 2, the mean template gap falls from 23.80 to 11.92 points; mean false rejection falls from 34.11% to 27.89%; reasoning accuracy rises from 64.92 to 67.44.

Transfer across GRPO and PPO

The same regularizer improves template robustness under both optimizers. Under PPO, the template gap falls from 17.41 to 5.73 points, while false rejection falls from 79.33% to 34.67%.

Regularization weights & token masking

The paper studies the SFT coefficient α, the RL coefficient λ, and consecutive-token masking length. Single-token masking offers the best safety/over-refusal balance in the reported comparison.

Extended safety-alignment baselines
Contrastive-data quality & evaluator agreement

The paper evaluates generated contrastive queries with independent model and human validators, and compares Llama Guard with human expert safety judgments on 150 instances.
