01THE MOTIVATION

When looking safe
is a shortcut.

Near-perfect safety scores can hide fragile behavior. We identify two superficial cues that pull safety alignment away from the underlying intent.

⌗FORMATTING SHORTCUT

Change the wrapper.
Lose the defense.

The instruction stays the same, but removing chat templates can sharply reduce defense success. The model has learned to rely on the wrapper.

04THE TAKEAWAY

Align to the intent.
Look beyond the cue.
